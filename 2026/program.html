Program

10th AWMS

Program

The program below is tentative and subject to change.

Plenary lecture: 50 min talk + 10 min Q&A
Invited talk (IT): 20 min talk + 5 min Q&A
Contributed talk (CT) and Student talk(ST): 15 min talk + 5 min Q&A

The student presentations (ST) are eligible for consideration for the Jon-Haugen Prize.

Poster: All poster presenters are expected to attend both poster sessions.
Poster board size is A0 (portrait).
The poster presentations given by students (marked with *) are eligible for consideration for the Li-Hong Xu Award.
