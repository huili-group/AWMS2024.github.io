Prize&Award

Prizes are awarded to young scientist for their outstanding presentations. Please remember that we highly value all contribution and that most participants will not receive a prize despite their great presentation at our meeting. The Jon Hougen Prize and the Li-Hong Xu Award were created to honor not only their great contributions to science but also their efforts in fostering communication across borders and among young scientists. The Asian Workshop on Molecular Spectroscopy would not exist without their tireless efforts and we will always remember their contributions. Any questions or suggestions about the Prizes should be addressed to the Conference Committee.

Prize and Award for AWMS 2026

 

Jon-Haugen Prize

 

The Jon-Haugen Prize recognizes outstanding student oral presentations. All presentations in the ST (Student) session are eligible for consideration.

 

Li-Hong Xu Award

 

The Li-Hong Xu Award recognizes outstanding poster presentations. Poster presentations marked with an asterisk (*) in the program are eligible for consideration.

 
Award Eligible Presentations
Jon-Haugen Prize All ST (Student) oral presentations
Li-Hong Xu Award Poster presentations marked with an asterisk (*)
