Prize&Award

Prizes are awarded to young scientists for their outstanding presentations. Please remember that we highly value all contributions and that many excellent presentations may not receive a prize. The Jon Hougen Prize and the Li-Hong Xu Award were created to honor not only their great contributions to science but also their efforts in fostering communication across borders and among young scientists. The Asian Workshop on Molecular Spectroscopy would not exist without their tireless efforts, and we will always remember their contributions. Any questions or suggestions about the prizes should be addressed to the Conference Committee.
