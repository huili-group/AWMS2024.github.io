10th AWMS

Introduction

The Asia-Pacific Workshop on Molecular Spectroscopy (AWMS) is committed to promoting academic exchanges between experimental and theoretical researchers worldwide in the field of molecular spectroscopy from all over the world, especially encouraging young scientists to present their latest work. The upcoming 10th AWMS is scheduled to take place at Hokkaido University, Sapporo, Japan on November 4 to November 6, 2026.

Theme

The themes that we intend to cover in this meeting are as follows:

  • Spectroscopy in gas phase
  • Application of spectroscopy to dynamics
  • New spectroscopic techniques
  • Precision measurement
  • Theoretical analysis
  • Spectroscopy of condensed phase/solid
  • AI for spectroscopy
  • Atmospheric and interstellar spectra
  • Other topics
