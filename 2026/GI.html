10th AWMS

Introduction

The Asian Workshop on Molecular Spectroscopy (AWMS) is committed to promoting academic exchanges between experimental and theoretical researchers worldwide in the field of molecular spectroscopy from all over the world, especially encouraging young scientists to present their latest work. The upcoming 10th AWMS is scheduled to take place at Hokkaido University, Sapporo, Japan on December 4 to December 6, 2026.
