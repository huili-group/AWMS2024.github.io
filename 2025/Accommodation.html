Accommodation

We recommend four hotels around the meeting place. Some of them would give special discounts for our attendees.

Coogee Bay Boutique Hotel

Coogee Bay Boutique Hotel is located at the conference site, Located only 20 minutes from the Sydney CBD and 15 minutes from the international airport, the Coogee Bay Hotel provides 74 boutique accommodation rooms. Surrounded by waterfront bars, restaurants and the iconic beach. Rooms start at an average of $265 (AUD).

Delegates can use this direct booking link for a discount off the best available rate

Location:253 Coogee Bay Road, Coogee, Sydney, NSW, 2034

Coogee Sands

Coogee Sands is a 4-minute walk to the conference venue, located right on Coogee Beach. Rooms start at an average of $250 (AUD).

Location:161-167 Dolphin Street, Coogee, Sydney, NSW, 2034

Adina Apartment Hotel Coogee, Sydney

Adina Apartment Hotel Coogee, Sydney is a 5-minute walk to the conference venue. Rooms start at an average of $304 (AUD).

Location:183 Coogee Bay Road, Coogee, Sydney, NSW, 2034

Dive Hotel Coogee Beach

Dive Hotel Coogee Beach is a 2-minute walk to the conference venue. This small boutique hotel is located on Coogee Beach. Rooms start at an average of $204 (AUD).

Location:234 Arden Street, Coogee, Sydney, NSW, 2034
